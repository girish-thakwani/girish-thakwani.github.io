    Carnegie Mellon University (CMU) · Pittsburgh, PA

    Carnegie Mellon University (CMU) · Pittsburgh, PA

  • 2025.09 - 2025.12
    Cross-Lingual Hallucination Detection in Multilingual LLMs
    Carnegie Mellon University (CMU) · Pittsburgh, PA
    • Tested whether correctness probes trained only on English hidden states of Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct can flag hallucinations zero-shot in Chinese, Thai, Vietnamese, and Indonesian; best probe reached 0.704 AUC on English and 0.64 to 0.68 across target languages with no target-language supervision.
    • Ran a full 28-layer sweep across six probe families and two extraction points, showing post-answer features and deeper layers transfer best and that the correctness signal is low-dimensional (random forests within 0.01 AUC of the MLP).
    • Traced the dominant failure to confident hallucinations the hidden state does not expose, and to conflating knowledge absence with hallucination, motivating a three-way correct/uncertain/hallucinating labeling scheme.
